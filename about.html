About Us

Independent media intelligence

A platform focused on understanding the changing media ecosystem across the Middle East and internationally.

Our Purpose

Media City Qatar is an independent media intelligence, analysis and industry information platform. It brings together developments from broadcasting, digital media, technology, media cities, innovation and the people shaping the sector.

Vision & Mission

Our vision is to provide a useful meeting point for media professionals, organisations and industry stakeholders. Our mission is to present informed industry analysis, highlight important developments and create a space for professional exchange.

Editorial Approach

Content should be informative, clearly presented and focused on the media industry. Analysis and opinion should be distinguished from reported information, with sources identified where appropriate.
